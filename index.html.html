01 // ABOUT

Currently an R&D Assistant Engineer — Embedded Systems & IoT at Cybernetics Hi-Tech Solutions (Pvt) Ltd in Dhaka, where I handle the hardware side of robotics: PCB assembly and repair, wiring harnesses, and firmware for ESP32, STM32, Arduino and Raspberry Pi boards that keep AGV, AMR and drone platforms running.

Before robotics, I spent years keeping heavier systems alive — substations, industrial generators, solar IPS and plant-wide power distribution as Lead Electrician for the Bangladesh Land Port Authority. That background shows up in how I build: a site engineer's respect for reliability, applied down at board level.
